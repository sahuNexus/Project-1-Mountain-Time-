Your checklist

Small preparations make a big difference.

Check the forecast

Review weather and road advisories before departure and each morning.

Pack layers

Carry warm layers, rain protection, sturdy footwear, water, and medication.

Allow extra time

Mountain roads are slow. Avoid night driving where possible.

Respect trail limits

Stay on marked paths and turn back if conditions worsen.

Leave no trace

Carry waste out and avoid disturbing wildlife or fragile habitats.

Share your plan

Tell someone your route and expected return, especially for hikes.
