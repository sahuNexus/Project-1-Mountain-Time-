Himachal Pradesh

Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala, and Dalhousie suit spring and summer. Manali also draws autumn visitors; winter brings cold and possible snow.

Himachal Tourism ↗

Uttarakhand

Kausani and Kanatal are popular in spring and summer for outdoor days. Autumn can be crisp; check route conditions.

Uttarakhand Tourism ↗

Kerala high ranges

Munnar is pleasant in cooler months. October-March is a useful trekking window; monsoon rain can restrict trails.

Kerala Tourism ↗

Darjeeling

April-June is popular for pleasant weather and mountain views. Check visibility and conditions.

Incredible India ↗

Nilgiri Hills

Ooty and Coonoor offer cool weather. Spring and early summer are popular; monsoon brings rain.

High-altitude routes

Remote routes may have short access seasons, permits, or guide requirements. Confirm before setting out.

Kashmir Valley

Srinagar, Gulmarg, and Pahalgam are lovely in spring and autumn. Summer is a popular time for pleasant weather; winter brings cold and possible snowfall.

JKTDC: seasons in Kashmir ↗

Jammu

Explore Jammu city and nearby hill escapes such as Patnitop. Spring and early summer suit outdoor sightseeing; check local weather and road updates year-round.

Jammu and Kashmir Tourism ↗

Ladakh

For trekking and high mountain routes, mid-May to mid-October is the broad season; June-September is especially suitable. Prepare for altitude and cold evenings.

District Leh: trekking season ↗

Maharashtra Sahyadris

Mahabaleshwar, Matheran, Lonavala, and Malshej Ghat turn lush in monsoon, with waterfalls and mist. For many hikes, October-February offers drier conditions.

Maharashtra Tourism: monsoon ↗

Western Ghats

Coorg, Ooty, Coonoor, and Munnar offer forests, plantations, and hill walks. October-March is a useful window for clearer outdoor days; monsoon is vivid but very wet.

Incredible India: Coorg seasons ↗

Eastern Ghats

Try Araku Valley, Yercaud, and Horsley Hills. October-May is a flexible broad window for exploring; choose cooler months for walks and check local rain conditions.

Incredible India: Eastern Ghats getaways ↗
