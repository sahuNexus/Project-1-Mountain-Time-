What kind of mountain trip?

Accommodation availability and services change by season. Confirm current details with the property before booking.

Homestays

A welcoming option for local food and a personal feel. Ask about heating, hot water, meals, and road access in winter.

Hotels and resorts

Useful for families or travellers who want on-site dining and services. Check what is included and how far it is from town.

Eco-lodges

Often close to nature and trails. Ask about waste management, water use, local staff, and wildlife-friendly practices.

Heritage stays

Find characterful old homes and properties in established hill stations. Check accessibility and heating for colder months.

Adventure camps

Best for guided outdoor trips where permits, gear, meals, and emergency support should be clearly confirmed.

Budget guesthouses

A practical base for independent travellers. Read recent reviews and check transport, bathrooms, and cancellation terms.

Where to stay

Pick a base that suits your itinerary.

For a town-based holiday, stay near the centre and local transport. For trekking, choose an approved lodge or homestay near the trailhead, and confirm access with local operators.

In high-altitude regions such as Ladakh, book acclimatization time into your trip and verify heating, medical access, and seasonal opening dates.
